Historical note date: 2026-05-25

## Generalized summary

The imported note reports the following interpretation:

Plyometric research primarily supports examining jumping, sprinting, and power outcomes; some studies also report muscle-size changes. Trial injury reporting and performance benefits do not establish safety at arbitrary landing volumes or equivalence to resistance training for hypertrophy.
