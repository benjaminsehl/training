## Generalized summary

The imported note reports the following interpretation:

Planned exercise variation and single- versus multi-joint comparisons address different questions. Whole-muscle growth may be similar across exercise classes, while regional targeting and strength specificity remain relevant; evidence does not support continual random exercise changes as inherently better.
