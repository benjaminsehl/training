## Generalized summary

The imported note reports the following interpretation:

The imported sleep-loss synthesis reports impaired performance across several domains and higher perceived effort. Sleep-extension evidence is smaller and less certain; neither literature establishes a personalized recovery rule or the effect of every isolated poor night.
