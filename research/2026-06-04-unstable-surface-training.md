# Unstable surface training

Historical note date: 2026-06-04  
Public summary prepared: 2026-10-12  
Status: imported research summary; underlying sources and bibliographic details not independently verified in this pass.

## Generalized summary

The imported note reports the following interpretation:

Unstable-surface exercise may improve balance, but its superiority to stable-surface resistance training depends on the task and population. Reduced external loading and limited direct hypertrophy evidence argue against inferring a general muscle-building advantage.
