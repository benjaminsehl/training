Historical note date: 2026-05-15

## Generalized summary

The imported note reports the following interpretation:

Direct comparisons report broadly similar muscle-growth outcomes for machines and free weights. Strength results depend strongly on the trained and tested modality, and an average null difference does not make every exercise interchangeable.
