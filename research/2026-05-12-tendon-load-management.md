## Generalized summary

The imported note reports the following interpretation:

Reviews in tendinopathy populations support progressive resistance loading, but do not identify a single best protocol across tendon conditions. Findings on load and frequency are limited by reporting quality and cannot be directly converted into injury-prevention or self-treatment instructions for healthy trainees.
