# Concurrent training interference

Historical note date: 2026-05-03  
Public summary prepared: 2026-10-12  
Status: imported research summary; underlying sources and bibliographic details not independently verified in this pass.

## Generalized summary

The imported note reports the following interpretation:

Concurrent endurance and resistance training was reported to preserve whole-muscle growth and maximal strength on average. Explosive strength and some muscle-fiber outcomes may be more sensitive; timing, training dose, and the measured outcome limit any blanket claim of compatibility.
