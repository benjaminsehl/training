## Generalized summary

The imported note reports the following interpretation:

Sequencing research separates whole-muscle growth, maximal strength, and explosive performance. Resistance-first ordering may favor lower-body dynamic strength within a shared session, while evidence for an ordering effect on hypertrophy is less decisive.
