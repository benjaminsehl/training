## Generalized summary

The imported note reports the following interpretation:

Volume-equated frequency comparisons suggest frequency alone has a limited effect on muscle growth. Distribution of weekly work may still affect practical session quality; earlier and later reviews differ in scope and should not be counted as independent confirmation of a fixed optimum.
