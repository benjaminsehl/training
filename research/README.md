## Evidence status

All entries are **imported and not independently source-verified in this pass**. A note date is its historical filing date, not proof of publication or source access on that date. Bibliographic attributions in the imported material were sometimes inconsistent; source locators are retained rather than repairing uncertain metadata by guesswork. Some references are abstracts, narrative reviews, expert commentary, preprints, or accepted manuscripts. These do not have the same evidentiary weight as direct trials or systematic reviews.

Repeated topics and repeated citations are preserved for dated-file portability, but are not independent replications. The [source catalogue](sources.json) consolidates exact duplicate URLs and selected clearly equivalent locators; further aliases may remain. The [file manifest](manifest.json) records the public summaries, dates, and checksums. Consult the repository's current evidence reviews and reusable skills for separately checked synthesis.

This collection offers research context, not medical care or an individualized program. Clinical-population findings, acute laboratory effects, and observational associations require particular care before application.
