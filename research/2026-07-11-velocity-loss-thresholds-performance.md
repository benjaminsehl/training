## Generalized summary

The imported note reports the following interpretation:

Lower velocity-loss thresholds were associated with better velocity or sprint outcomes in the cited lower-body reviews. Their comparatively narrow performance evidence does not establish the optimal threshold for hypertrophy or all exercises.
