# Deloads periodization fatigue

Historical note date: 2026-05-18  
Public summary prepared: 2026-10-12  
Status: imported research summary; underlying sources and bibliographic details not independently verified in this pass.

## Generalized summary

The imported note reports the following interpretation:

Volume-matched periodization research reports clearer support for strength than for additional muscle growth. A separate training-cessation trial cannot resolve the effects of reduced-volume deloads, longer blocks, or different fatigue-management strategies.
