## Generalized summary

The imported note reports the following interpretation:

The imported blood-pressure reviews report average reductions from regular resistance training, mainly in hypertensive or clinical populations. Heterogeneity and risk of bias limit individual prediction; resting or ambulatory changes do not resolve acute lifting safety or replace clinical care.
