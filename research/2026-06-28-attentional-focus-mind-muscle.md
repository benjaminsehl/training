# Attentional focus mind muscle

Historical note date: 2026-06-28

## Generalized summary

The imported note reports the following interpretation:

Attentional-focus studies suggest that external cues can aid immediate force output, while direct growth evidence is sparse and muscle-specific. Acute performance findings and one small internal-focus hypertrophy trial should not be merged into a universal cueing rule.
