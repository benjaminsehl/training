# Progressive push up calisthenics

Historical note date: 2026-07-19  
Public summary prepared: 2026-10-12  
Status: imported research summary; underlying sources and bibliographic details not independently verified in this pass.

## Generalized summary

The imported note reports the following interpretation:

Small, short push-up studies suggest that adequately progressed bodyweight pressing can produce strength and, in some trials, muscle-size gains. The evidence is mainly pressing-specific and should not be extrapolated to advanced leverage skills or all calisthenics.
