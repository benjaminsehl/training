## Generalized summary

The imported note reports the following interpretation:

Higher velocity loss usually entails more repetitions and acute fatigue, so comparisons can confound fatigue threshold with accumulated work. The cited reviews suggest goal-dependent performance tradeoffs rather than a universal percentage for strength, power, and hypertrophy.
