## Generalized summary

The imported note reports the following interpretation:

The cited network meta-analysis reports benefits from many resistance-training prescriptions compared with no exercise. A separate minimal-dose overview describes lower-burden options, but establishing that a small dose helps is different from showing that it maximizes growth.
