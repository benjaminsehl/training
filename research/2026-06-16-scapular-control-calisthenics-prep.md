## Generalized summary

The imported note reports the following interpretation:

Shoulder-therapy trials and scapular muscle-activation studies concern different populations and outcomes. Improved function in symptomatic patients or higher acute activation cannot establish injury prevention, superior hypertrophy, or readiness for demanding calisthenics.
