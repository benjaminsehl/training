## Generalized summary

The imported note reports the following interpretation:

The imported rest review suggests a small growth advantage above very short rest intervals, with uncertain differences among longer rests. Failure to detect a benefit beyond a threshold is not proof of equivalence, and preserving subsequent-set performance remains a separate consideration.
