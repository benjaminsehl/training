## Generalized summary

The imported note reports the following interpretation:

Feedback and supervision studies report acute performance and some longer-term strength benefits. Direct hypertrophy evidence is thinner, and changes in effort, participation, and execution may contribute; findings do not identify one universally necessary feedback format.
