## Generalized summary

The imported note reports the following interpretation:

The imported reviews favor considering tension at longer muscle lengths, while leaving superiority of lengthened partials over full-range repetitions unsettled. Findings vary by muscle and exercise, and a proposed mechanism is not proof of universal benefit.
