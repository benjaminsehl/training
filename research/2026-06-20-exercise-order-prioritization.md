## Generalized summary

The imported note reports the following interpretation:

The exercise-order meta-analysis reports greater strength gains in movements trained earlier. Pooled hypertrophy differences are less clear; ordering can therefore be understood as task prioritization without claiming that one sequence always produces more growth.
