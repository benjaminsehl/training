# Foam rolling warmup recovery

Historical note date: 2026-06-29  
Public summary prepared: 2026-10-12  
Status: imported research summary; underlying sources and bibliographic details not independently verified in this pass.

## Generalized summary

The imported note reports the following interpretation:

Foam-rolling research describes possible short-term mobility or perceived-recovery benefits without a clear special performance advantage over other warm-ups. Subjective soreness relief does not establish faster tissue recovery or additional hypertrophy.
