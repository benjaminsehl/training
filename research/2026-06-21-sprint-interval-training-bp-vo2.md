## Generalized summary

The imported note reports the following interpretation:

A small cycling-based sprint-interval comparison reports improvements in aerobic fitness and some blood-pressure outcomes with both interval and continuous training. Between-group and subgroup estimates are uncertain and do not establish a treatment protocol or transfer automatically to other exercise modes.
