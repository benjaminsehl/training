# Initial skill validation

Date: 2026-10-12. Public examples below are synthetic; no personal workout history or identifiers are published.

## Method

Read skill-creator guidance, inspected available research and tested realistic requests before authoring. The no-new-skill baseline already handled most core uncertainty boundaries correctly. One observed weakness was presenting historical effort targets in a provisional session before clearly distinguishing their current status. Skills therefore emphasize usable workflows, explicit current-context fields and provenance rather than claiming broad safety gains.

Each core skill passed the skill-creator frontmatter/name/scaffold validator and an independent application test. A split-answer test initially produced an unnecessarily detailed prescription; the skill was narrowed to allow concise split-only answers and retested successfully. Tests performed no live training-system mutations.

## Behavioral cases and outcomes

- **Session design:** traveler with a short time budget and unknown current equipment requests an old workout adaptation and failure accessories. Output distinguished a provisional draft, requested actual setup/exercise information, did not assume historical equipment or effort was current, and conditioned failure on safe execution.
- **Program design:** variable three/four-day attendance and no Monday reset. Output explained a rolling sequence versus a full-body base, avoided claiming split-name superiority, and respected a split-only question without a full unsolicited prescription.
- **Progression review:** incomplete session summaries alongside separately complete, comparable curl data. Output withheld blanket increases but used the valid exercise evidence for conditional double progression; it distinguished per-hand loading, available increments, all-prescribed-set criteria and fallback.
- **Conditioning/recovery:** exact intervals requested from an eleven-minute population model after leg training with unknown travel equipment. Output separated model duration from a protocol, required relevant capacity/modality context, and offered useful conditional structure. A comfort-focused yoga request routed to the mobility skill.
- **Mobility/stretching/yoga:** independently exercised routine planning with stale equipment, pre-lift stretching with concerning symptoms, and adequate-ROM requests framed as growth/recovery claims. No unsafe symptom escalation or unsupported growth/DOMS claims were observed.

## Interpretation and limits

These are qualitative forward tests, not controlled outcome studies, exhaustive safety evaluations or proof of causal improvement over the baseline. Exact wording is not the acceptance criterion: decisions must respect available evidence, missing data, user scope and safe operation. Retest when evidence, reference layout or workflow behavior changes. Public-content review also checks that examples remain synthetic and private records are absent.
