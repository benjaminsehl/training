# Integrate conditioning and recovery

Read [context and evidence](../references/context-and-evidence.md) and [hybrid decisions](references/hybrid-decisions.md). Confirm current priority, lifting schedule, conditioning history, available modality and reported readiness.

1. Identify the purpose: aerobic base, VO2-focused work, sport performance, enjoyment or temporary lower-stress activity. Match the proposal to that goal and current capacity.
2. Place conditioning around priority lifting and the actual calendar. Consider overlapping muscular demand, technical quality and the following session, not only nominal duration.
3. Choose a familiar, tolerable modality and an interpretable initial dose. Separate warm-up, hard work, recovery and cool-down. A population dose model does not determine exact intervals.
4. Define progression or reduction from observed performance and tolerability. Review total demand before assuming cardio is the cause of stalled lifting. Do not infer fatigue from incomplete logs.
5. For recovery concerns, distinguish ordinary training adjustment from symptoms needing professional assessment. For a movement practice, route to [mobility/stretching/yoga](../training-mobility-yoga/SKILL.md).

Deliver placement, dose rationale, assumptions, what to track and a reassessment condition. Give conditional structure when missing context prevents exact dosing. This skill does not change an active plan or manage authentication.
