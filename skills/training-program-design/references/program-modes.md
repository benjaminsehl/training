# Program modes

Use only the mode relevant to the request. These modes share a design workflow; they do not require separate thin skills.

## Hypertrophy-first

Define priority muscles and sustainable training opportunities. Select repeatable exercises that allow the intended muscle work with tolerable setup and fatigue. Start from recent completed, recoverable work; preserve progression comparability. Judge a proposed increase by repeated training quality and recovery, with appropriate longer-term body-composition/measurement context, not one pump or session PR. [Failure evidence](../../../evidence/2026-10-05-failure-rir.md) does not require every set to fail. Optimal personal volume remains unresolved.

## Strength emphasis

Name the task to improve: a particular lift/variation, rep strength or testing outcome. Preserve specific practice and compare the same execution standard. Arrange assistance to support the priority task without making every session a max test. Stronger frequency associations in [reviewed evidence](../../references/source-checks-2026-10-12.md) do not dictate daily heavy training. A peaking schedule or percentage-based block requires valid current performance and the actual target date; do not invent a 1RM from an uncalibrated effort label.

## Time-constrained or maintenance

Clarify whether the user still wants growth or temporarily wants to preserve capacity. A reduced-dose fallback is not a promise of equal growth. Keep a feasible exposure to important patterns and quality execution, reduce optional work, and define how normal training resumes. Do not accumulate make-up sessions after disruption. If illness, injury or restrictions caused the interruption, their constraints take precedence over maintaining intensity. A personal maintenance minimum is not established by this skill; consult relevant source evidence when the exact dose matters.

## Minimal equipment or calisthenics

Use current location inventory. Track variation, leverage, assistance, range and external load rather than treating all push-ups/pull-ups as the same exercise. Map target roles and acknowledge unfilled coverage; do not call any bodyweight swap equivalent to a loadable machine pattern. Keep new skill practice distinguishable from hard resistance work. When mobility/yoga is the requested outcome, use [training-mobility-yoga](../../training-mobility-yoga/SKILL.md) rather than relabeling resistance work.

## Named programs

Distinguish faithful implementation from adaptation. If exact details of a named commercial program matter, obtain the actual authorized source. This skill does not reproduce commercial program tables. A source program's phase-specific failure rules, fixed deload weeks and advanced methods do not transfer automatically to a different block. Explain deviations and their purpose instead of combining incompatible templates under the original name.
